# Idea

What is rabbit hole management?
https://chatgpt.com/share/68f3c959-e81c-8008-b59e-7bd5530632cf

Strategies for rabbit hole management
- Use timers
* Bookmark or jot down ideas
* Set clear intentions before starting
* Leverage it.

What makes managing rabbit holes tricky?
1. Time Blindness
2. Task switching paralysis
3. Overwhelm and unfinished projects
4. Neglect of priorities
5. Mental exhaustion and crash
6. Difficulty retracing thought paths
7. Emotional fallout

Consequences of rabbit holes:
- Waste of time can lead to guilt and shame.
* Forget to have food on time.
* If the person continues to hyper-focus on a task beyond far beyond their regular bedtime, it may lead to difficulty sleeping in later hours and can eventually, ruin the next day.
* Forget about the deadlines, obligations etc.

Pitch:
An application for managing rabbit holes It's basically, a combination of typical todo-list applications and [dpwrk](https://www.dpwrk.app/) app based on ADHD challenges. Here is what the application should do:
- They could start a pomodoro with the time they estimate for a particular task.
* Before starting pomodoro they should breakdown their intention for the session as clearly as possible. Allowing rich text for this so that it can be bullet points, checklist, or a set of numbered instructions to be followed in an order. We may use Natural language to verify their pomodoro goal is clear (may be follow the SMART goal acronym or any other criteria to ensure the goal is clear and achievable, so that we can hold them accountable for the task).
* If needed user should be able to use a specific white/brown noise for their pomodoro session.
* Once the pomodoro opens, we can minimize our application into a small widget, which has 3 icons mute sound if needed, ticking time visual, a chat icon. This chat icon should let them jot down the ideas/questions/curiosities/wierd thoughts coming in between the session. The app will block out sites and any application not relevant to the specified intention.
* If the user completes the task before the estimated time, we should give them boosts. Asking them to upload a screenshot or pic of what they accomplished will be helpful to hold them more accountable, but this should be optional and personalizable.
* Incase they want to pause the pomodoro due to any external distraction/emergency, we should ask what is the distraction/what they are planning to do for that pause and set a reminder for that. For example, collect delivery item, will be back in 5 minutes (or my mind is not in zone right now will be back after a short walk of 10 minutes). An alarm will ring if the user doesn't come back in 5 minutes or 10 minutes.
*  In case of emergency they want to leave the session before the time, they should report the things accomplished and put any breadcrumbs(breadcrumbs are essentially, piece of information related to task they were working on to help them continue a paused task when they are back)
* Incase they have high energy or more attention then should be allowed to extend pomodoro timing in a healthy way. There should be cut before they should move or take a break. Also, the extension should consider their daily food time and sleep times and other time sensitive obligations so that their hyper-focus doesn't creep into their not negotiable stuffs (health, relationship and any time sensitive commitments)
* Once the intended task is completed they should be asked whether they want to review their parking lots (notes) now or assign that to an intentional rabbit hole time (mentioned in point no 4 in personalization).
* While review they can create a task from their notes or add as notes to any folder or something. We can enable them to search for these tasks or notes or ask AI to declutter it for them if needed.

Personalization
1. Daily food time windows: Ask the users about their food timings. Its basically, the time window they eat their breakfast, lunch and dinner. This will vary for individuals based on their chronotype/circadian rhythm/work shifts. A person who wakes up late will be taking food late etc.
2. Sleeping window. This will allow us to focus at max 30 minutes before the bedtime.
3. Collect the time sensitive things at the start of each day. If there are any recurring events/activities allow them to add that as well. For recurring tasks, if needed can set multiple reminders to break out of hyper-focus.
4. Ask them to take a short walk, drink break, stretch or even nap in between task based on personalization.
5. Intentional rabbit holes: Set aside time each day/week based on user preferences. If they have commute time, it can be utilized for random wikipedia deep dive or allowing their mind to wander behind their curiosities.
6. Tracking the common distractions can help the users to recalibrate their strategies.

Considerations
- Add palette cleansers: ADHD people may need more time for context switching between tasks.
* ADHD brains respond well with rewards. Linking completion of task with a reward should help. It can be an external reward. For example, we may give user some points based on their task completions. Upon reaching a certain level they may unlock their personal reward goals
